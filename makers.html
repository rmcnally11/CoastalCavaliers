Who we buy from

Every name on the box is a neighbour.

We buy from people we have stood in the kitchen with. When you read a name on a jar, that is who made it and where. We are signing the first makers now — no invented baker profiles, no stock photos of strangers.

Bakers

Registered Texas cottage kitchens

Loaves pulled the morning of a run, from people baking out of a converted garage rather than a factory. Their name goes on the label, because the law requires it and because it is the best thing about the box.

The deal, plainly

You set the price. You cap the run.

  • You set the price. Your price is what the member pays. Our fee sits beside it, visible, never taken out of yours.
  • Nothing on spec. You get a purchase order Wednesday for exactly what sold. You never bake into the dark.
  • Paid every Friday, with a one‑page statement. Cottage makers pay 0% for the first ninety days, then 15%, stepping down to 12.5% at volume.
  • You cap your own capacity. Tell us sixty loaves and you will never see an order for sixty‑one.
  • No listing fee, ever. Not to join, not to be featured, not to be seen. We only earn when we sell your work.
