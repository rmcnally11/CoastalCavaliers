For makers

Zero fees.Friday pay.

You are twenty miles inland on a Saturday morning. The people with money and empty coolers are standing on a dock. We are the road between the two, and we do not charge you for the privilege of being on it.

Who we buy from

Every name on the box is a neighbour.

We buy from people we have stood in the kitchen with. When you read a name on a jar, that is who made it and where. We are signing the first makers now — no invented baker profiles, no stock photos of strangers.

Bakers

Registered cottage kitchens

Loaves pulled the morning of a run, from people baking out of a converted garage rather than a factory. Their name goes on the label.

Coastal brands

Licensed small-batch producers

Smoked fish, farmstead cheese, sauces from inspected kitchens — six-person operations, not corporations.

Roasters & the pantry

Cottage and licensed

Coffee roasted on a two-kilo drum. Honey from hives behind a field. Pantry items that make a galley feel stocked.

The deal, plainly

You set the price. You cap the run.

  • You set the price. Your price is what the member pays. Our fee sits beside it, visible, never taken out of yours.
  • Nothing on spec. You get a purchase order Wednesday for exactly what sold.
  • Paid every Friday, with a one-page statement. Cottage makers: 0% for ninety days, then 15%.
  • You cap your own capacity. Tell us sixty loaves and you will never see an order for sixty-one.
  • No listing fee, ever. We only earn when we sell your work.
