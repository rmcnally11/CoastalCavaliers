The covenant

We do not eat the channel.

We never wet-hose at a marina with a working fuel dock, unless that marina asks — outage, winter hours, overflow, at the dock’s own posted price. A hose on somebody else’s pumps is how you lose every wholesale conversation in the basin.

A hose is not a bread box. Waterdog is its own company. The books do not mix.
