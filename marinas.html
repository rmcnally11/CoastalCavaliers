We bring the cooler, the insurance and the indemnity. You give us a corner out of the sun and a mention in the newsletter. Your slip holders get something no marina down the channel offers, and everyone who collects a box walks past your register. We are not announcing invented partnerships — we are asking for the first visits now.

  • You pay nothing. Take a flat monthly fee, a per‑box fee, or nothing at all and treat it as an amenity.
  • You staff nothing. We supply and service the cooler. Your only job is to not unplug it.
  • Zero liability by design. Our product liability names your marina as additional insured, with a full indemnity and a certificate before the first box.
  • Month to month. Thirty days’ notice, either side, no cause required.
